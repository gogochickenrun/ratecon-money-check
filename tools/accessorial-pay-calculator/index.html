Why accessorial revenue gets lost

Small amounts are easy to forget: a lumper receipt here, two hours of detention there, an approved extra stop in a text thread. The invoice should reflect every approved charge with the proof the broker requires.

Do not use the calculator to invent charges

The purpose is to total written or approved accessorials. Check the actual Rate Con for eligibility, notice requirements, receipts, timestamps and caps.
